Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Relay, how it works, and how to get started.

What is Relay (RelayChat)?

Relay (also known as RelayChat) is a modern, distraction-free messaging application built specifically for one-on-one communication. Unlike standard messaging platforms filled with public channels, group spam, and news feeds, Relay keeps every interaction direct, fast, and personal.

Is Relay completely free to use?

Yes, Relay is 100% free to sign up and use. There are no premium paywalls, hidden charges, or artificial message limits.

How can I access or download Relay on my device?

Relay is accessible across all major devices. You can download our native Android app (APK) directly from this page, or use Relay in any web browser. Relay is also a Progressive Web App (PWA), so you can install it directly onto your iPhone, iPad, Android, Mac, or Windows home screen.

What makes Relay different from other chat apps?

Relay eliminates unnecessary clutter. There are no public channels or algorithms competing for your attention. It delivers instant real-time synchronization, online presence dots, typing indicators, read receipts, and an integrated AI assistant in a clean, dark-themed interface.

How does the built-in AI Assistant work?

Relay includes a dedicated AI companion accessible right from your chat list. You can ask questions, draft messages, translate text, or get quick answers anytime without leaving your conversation environment.

How do I find friends or contacts on Relay?

Finding contacts is fast. Use the search bar in Relay to search for friends by their name or unique @username. Selecting their profile immediately opens your direct 1-on-1 chat room.

Is my conversation data safe and private?

Yes. Your privacy is paramount. Relay uses secure cloud backend rules ensuring that message data is strictly accessible only by the conversation participants. We do not track users across the web or sell data to advertisers.
